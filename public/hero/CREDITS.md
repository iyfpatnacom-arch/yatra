## What to do about the three marked ⚠️

They are served from `/public`, so no hotlinking and no dependency on the
source sites — but that does not grant a licence. Before this page goes live,
either replace them with photographs the temple owns (the yatra takes these
same photos every year), or get written permission from each source.

`nandgaon.jpg` is the urgent one: the photographer's watermark is baked into
the bottom-left of the frame and will be visible on the site.
